# CAS writing companion

A portable instruction file for developing example IB Creativity, Activity, Service (CAS) descriptions, goals and reflections from supplied experience details and the current student's own writing samples.

**[Read or download SKILL.md](SKILL.md)**

## How to use it

1. Attach `SKILL.md` to an AI conversation, or paste its contents, and explicitly ask the assistant to follow it.
2. Provide one original reflection, description or short paragraph you wrote yourself. More samples can improve the style match, but one is enough to start. Without a sample, the assistant can offer a neutral draft without claiming to match your voice.
3. Describe the experience, what you personally did or plan to do, and whether you want a description, goals, a reflection or an edit.
4. Answer a few focused questions or choose a draft with clearly marked gaps when information is missing.
5. Review the example and the selected learning outcomes. Correct facts, fill gaps and check your school's requirements.

The file can be supplied as instructions to Codex, ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. It does not install tools, provide permanent memory or grant access to external services.

## What the skill includes

- Descriptions, goals, reflections and targeted edits.
- Broad style matching based on the current student's original writing, without copying sentence templates or importing another student's experiences.
- Automatic selection of supported CAS learning outcomes, with a separate outcome section by default.
- Clear distinctions between plans, participation and demonstrated learning.
- Questions or visible placeholders for missing details, with no invented activities, emotions, achievements, hours or community impact.
- A built-in review of factual grounding, CAS fit, style, clarity and request compliance.
- An optional, authorised external detector workflow with a finite revision budget. Detector access is not required for the main writing workflow, and a reported score is not proof of authorship or approval for submission.

The skill does not submit work to ManageBac or contact supervisors. Students remain responsible for verifying facts and following their school's AI-use and acknowledgment rules.

## References and scope

Links to the IB's public CAS guidance, ManageBac guidance and other reference material are included in `SKILL.md`. The instructions are self-contained for ordinary use. The optional research companion mentioned in the skill is not required and is not included here. Personal writing samples and local trial files are not distributed in this repository.

This is an independent resource, not an official IB or ManageBac publication. It does not guarantee that an experience is eligible or that an entry satisfies a particular school's requirements.
